M2 · Placement grid you can act on

Placement is the first thing an operator reads on the Dashboard, but each cell is a coloured span with a tooltip: it cannot be focused, cannot be clicked, does not show proxied copies, and says up or down by hue alone. This plan makes every cell a keyboard-reachable button with a glyph, adds a column per egress, and puts the fix one keypress away.

Mockup, dark theme, data fake. Colours and type from app/static/css/style.css on origin/main. Purple numbered circles are notes for the reader.

BEFORE The grid today

Restarting Honeygain on box-b, the day it dies

  • Cells are <span class="cell cell-fail" title=…> (app.js:1481-1485): no tabindex, no click handler. Tab skips the whole grid (screenshot 33-kbd-dash-tab15.png).
  • To act: scroll to the Services table, find Honeygain, expand its copies, find the box-b row, click Restart (app.js:2079-2141): 1 scroll + visual search + 2-3 clicks, the cause (exit code, OOM) is not on the way.
  • State is colour only: .cell-ok green vs .cell-fail red differ by luminance 1.16:1 (style.css:1433-1435), so red-green colour-blind users see two equal squares.
  • Proxied copies are dropped before placement sees them (orchestrator.py:3034-3038): a dead copy behind isp-1 never shows on the Dashboard.
  • A service whose account is disabled still shows green cells and an Up count; nothing says the copies earn nothing.
  • No “last updated” time: a grid frozen by a failed poll looks the same as a fresh one.
