What each earner container does on the network: how many connections it opens, how many fail, which ports and networks it talks to, and whether it reaches into your own network or sends mail. It is off until you switch it on for a box. Every 5 minutes the box starts one short-lived helper per earner that reads the kernel's socket tables and connection counters. It never reads what is inside the traffic, the names looked up, or the sites visited.

Connection counts are complete; destinations are sampled (5 snapshots over 8 s every 5 min), so short connections are counted but may be missing from the list. UDP without a fixed peer shows no destination. Remote addresses are kept for 24 hours, the totals by port, network and country for 35 days. Everything stays on this server; network names come from a table downloaded to it. How it works
