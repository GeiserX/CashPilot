Owner account

This account has full access: it adds services, workers and other users.

Shown once in the container logs when CashPilot first started, on a line beginning “Setup token to create the owner account”. Run docker logs cashpilot-ui to see it. It proves you are the person who installed this, so nobody else can claim the owner account first.
