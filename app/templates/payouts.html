These services keep a node identity, a keystore or a wallet on disk, and nothing else holds a copy. CashPilot refuses to delete these volumes, but it cannot protect them from a wiped disk, a rebuilt machine or docker volume rm. Keep a copy somewhere else, then mark each box as backed up.
